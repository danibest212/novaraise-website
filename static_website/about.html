Who we are

Novaraise Digital Solution PLC is an Ethiopian technology company. We build software, AI and data solutions for organizations in Ethiopia, North America and Europe, and we train and employ the Ethiopian professionals who do the work.

Our mission

To make Ethiopia a trusted source of digital and AI talent, and to create skilled, fairly paid jobs through every project we deliver.

FOUNDATIONAL PRINCIPLES

What We Believe

Quality first.

We agree on what good looks like before beginning, and we deliver to it with rigorous testing.

Honest scope and price.

No surprises. What is agreed is what is delivered, without sudden hidden fees.

Local language matters.

Technology should work in the languages people actually speak: Amharic, Oromo, Tigrinya, and Somali.

Jobs are the point.

Our growth is measured by the real, verified, dignified economic opportunities we create in Ethiopia.
