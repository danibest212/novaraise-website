THE REALITY OF FIELD REPORTING

The Problem

Every donor-funded project must report, again and again. Field staff collect data in notebooks, spreadsheets and chat apps. Photos, attendance lists and signatures are chased by hand.

Reports are rewritten for each funder. Weeks are lost, and a weak or late report can put funding at risk.

Lost photos & paper signatures
Weeks spent reconciling spreadsheets
Risk of delayed grant disbursements
SEAMLESS FIELD WORKFLOW

How ReportReady Works

1. Record.

Field staff log an activity on their phone, even without internet. Photos, GPS location, attendance and signatures are attached. Everything syncs when they are back online.

OFFLINE SYNC
2. Track.

Managers see all activities and evidence on a live dashboard across regions, projects, and implementing partners in real time.

CENTRAL TELEMETRY
3. Check.

Missing-evidence alerts show what is not yet proven, so gaps are fixed before a report goes out to the donor.

ZERO GAPS
4. Report.

Generate the report in the funder's template, in English, Amharic or Oromo, ready for your review and approval.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does it work without internet?

Yes. Data is saved securely on the phone and synced automatically when network connectivity is restored.

Can it use our donor's report format?

We set up and customize the template with you during onboarding to match your specific funder requirements.

Who owns our data?

You do. You retain 100% complete ownership and control of all field data, photos, signatures, and reports.
