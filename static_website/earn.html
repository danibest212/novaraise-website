How It Works

1. Sign up.

Create your free account using your phone number. No fees, ever.

2. Take a short test.

Show that you know your language through a quick 5-minute reading or typing check.

3. Start tasks.

Record sentences, transcribe audio, translate or check others' work on your phone.

4. Get paid.

Earnings are sent directly to your Telebirr account or bank transfer upon validation.

Who Can Join

Anyone 18 or older who speaks, reads and writes one of our languages (Amharic, Afaan Oromoo, Tigrinya, Somali) well and has a smartphone.

Students, graduates, women, and people outside the big cities are all welcome to apply and contribute.

Your Rights

  • You always know what a task is and what you will be paid before starting.
  • Your personal information and privacy are strictly protected.
  • You can leave at any time with all earned funds paid out.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much can I earn?

Pay depends on the task length and type. The exact rate in ETB is shown transparently before you start each task.

How soon am I paid?

Payouts are processed regularly via Telebirr or direct bank transfer once quality review criteria are verified.

Is it free to join?

Yes. Never pay anyone to join Novaraise. Registration and qualification are 100% free.

Ready to start earning?

Enter your details to join the contributor pool. We will send you an onboarding SMS and test link.
